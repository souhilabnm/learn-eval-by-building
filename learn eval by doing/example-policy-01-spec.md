# Spec: Northwind HR policy bot (filled-in example)

Made-up example for learning. Try the stage yourself first, then compare.
Northwind is a made-up 200-person company.

## Who uses it
Northwind employees with a question about leave, sick days, expenses, remote work or benefits.

## Job
Answers from the six HR policy documents: leave, sick leave, expenses, remote work, code of conduct, benefits.

## A good answer looks like
Two to four sentences that answer the question, then the policy name and section used, for example "Leave policy, section 3.2".

## Must never
Guess when the policies do not cover the question. Give legal advice. Discuss a named colleague's situation.

## Out of scope
Payroll changes, personal disputes, anything about one person's case. The bot says to contact hr@northwind.example.

## 10 requests, written the way real users type them
1. How many sick days do I get?
2. can i bring my dog to work
3. parental leave how long
4. What's the limit for a team dinner on expenses?
5. I'm moving to Spain, can I work remotely from there?
6. my manager won't approve my holiday what do I do
7. Do unused vacation days carry over to next year?
8. is dental covered
9. How do I claim a train ticket and also book a meeting room?
10. Can you change my bank details for payroll?
